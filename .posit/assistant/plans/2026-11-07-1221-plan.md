## Known constraints carrying over

- Joint (weak + strong + activities) set-ESEM is inadmissible (min eigenvalue −.13; weak–strong same-factor residual correlations ≥ 1.0). Main models are the two separate set-ESEM models. The text must say why (cross-tie factor redundancy, same failure under CFA and ESEM specifications).
- `target.strict` is unsupported in lavaan 0.7-3's `efa()` (silently downgraded to PST with a warning). Off-target loadings are pulled toward zero, not pinned (max off-target |loading|: .41 weak, .36 strong). This must be described honestly in the text.
- EFA-block factor covariances are rotation-determined; disturbance covariances exist only across blocks (network factors within a block still get their covariances).
- Gender items remain excluded; gender-exclusion footnote carries over (no-gender refit numbers unchanged).
- The two "sensitive" indirect results flagged in memory (weak leisure aggregate; strong conservatism leisure aggregate) are pending a bootstrap — do the bootstrap in this round since the decompositions are being rebuilt anyway.
- The paper's coding statement (education ordinal, age linear in years) matches the SEM and stays.
